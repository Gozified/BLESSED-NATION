PASTOR CHIBUIKE DIVINE NWAFOR, Founder & President, Strength Embassy Global Gospel Center

Pastor Chibuike Divine Nwafor is the visionary founder and lead pastor of Strength Embassy Global Gospel Center (a.k.a. Blessed Nation), officially abbreviated as STEM. Born in 1992 in Ishielu LGA of Ebonyi State, Nigeria, he carries a divine mandate to raise a people of strength through the gospel of Christ.

He holds a degree in Industrial Chemistry from Ebonyi State University and further advanced academically at the University of Benin, where he earned a Master’s degree in Inorganic Chemistry and pursued a PhD in Medical Chemistry from 2018 to 2025. His life reflects a rare blend of scientific excellence and deep spiritual devotion.

In obedience to the instruction of the Holy Ghost, he founded the ministry in 2020, with its first service held on August 30th. The church has undergone Spirit-led name transitions—from Oaks House Church (Saviours Chapel), to Oaks Embassy Int’l Church (Covenant City), to Oaks Embassy Int’l Church (Blessed Nation), and finally to Strength Embassy Global Gospel Center.

Pastor Chibuike is also a prolific author and publisher of several bestselling books, including The Blessings of the Lord, The Power of Righteousness, Healing Scriptures, Victory Strategies, Your Complete Salvation, and How God Speaks. Through his teachings, writings, and leadership, God continues to bless and transform lives around the world.

He is committed to the kingdom mandate with passion and humility. As at the time of this writing, he has not yet married, devoting himself wholly to the work of the ministry and the spread of the gospel.

Contemporary Biography of Pastor Chibuike Divine Nwafor

Pastor Chibuike Divine Nwafor was born in 1992 in Ishielu Local Government Area of Ebonyi State, Nigeria. From an early age, he developed a passion for knowledge, personal growth, and the things of the Spirit. This passion guided his academic path and later shaped his calling in ministry.

He pursued Industrial Chemistry at Ebonyi State University, Nigeria, where he earned his first degree. With a strong desire to expand his scientific understanding, he advanced to the University of Benin, Edo State, obtaining a Master’s degree in Inorganic Chemistry. His academic journey continued at the same university, where he majored in Medical Chemistry at the PhD level, a program he embarked on from 2018 to 2025. Throughout this period, he maintained an uncommon balance between rigorous scientific study and deep spiritual commitment.

In 2020, under a clear and compelling instruction of the Holy Ghost, Pastor Chibuike founded a church ministry. The first service was held on August 30th, 2020, marking the dawn of a vision that would grow and transform lives. The ministry experienced several Spirit-led name transitions as its mandate expanded:

  1. Oaks House Church (a.k.a. Saviours Chapel)
  2. Oaks Embassy Int’l Church (a.k.a. Covenant City)
  3. Oaks Embassy Int’l Church (a.k.a. Blessed Nation)

And finally, its present and established identity:

Strength Embassy Global Gospel Center (a.k.a. Blessed Nation)
Officially abbreviated as STEM

THE MESSAGE WE CARRY: A KINGDOM MANDATE FOR TRANSFORMATION

The heartbeat of our ministry is centered on one unchanging truth: Jesus is Lord, and His Kingdom rules over all. Every message we preach flows from this foundation and is designed to build believers, transform families, raise kingdom stewards, and extend Christ’s influence to the nations. Our assignment is not merely to gather people, but to form disciples who walk in faith, demonstrate the character of Christ, and carry His power into every sphere of life.

1. The Message of Faith: The Believer’s Ultimate Shield.

Faith is the believer’s shield and the safest atmosphere. It is the force that pleases God, unlocks the supernatural, and gives us victory in all circumstances. Our teachings anchor believers in the integrity of God’s Word, helping them develop unshakeable trust in His promises.

2. Blessings: Living in God’s Covenant Reality

We emphasize the blessing of the Lord that makes rich and adds no sorrow. Every child of God has been positioned to enjoy covenant benefits—provision, protection, promotion, and peace. Our message unveils how to walk consciously in these blessings.

3. Family Life: Building Godly Homes

A strong church is built on strong families. We teach God’s pattern for marriage, parenting, and relationships, empowering believers to build homes filled with love, honour, communication, and purpose. When families thrive, society is healed.

4. Glory (The supernatural life). Is The Believer’s Heritage!

God designed every believer to radiate His glory, command his glory and to walk in the supernatural naturally. Our teachings awaken the consciousness of divine identity, showing believers that they are carriers of God’s presence and ambassadors of His greatness on the earth.

5. Power, Riches, Wisdom and Strength: Dimensions of Kingdom Expression.

We preach the full counsel of God revealed in Revelation 5:12.

  • • Power to live victoriously and advance the Kingdom.
  • • Riches to enable kingdom exploits and break the spirit of poverty.
  • • Wisdom for strategic living, problem-solving, and influence.
  • • Strength to endure, build, and finish God’s assignment. These dimensions equip believers to excel spiritually and practically.

6. Stewardship: Managing God’s Resources with Purpose.

Everything we have—time, gifts, resources, opportunities, belongs to God. Our teachings prepare believers to live responsibly, give generously, serve faithfully, and advance God’s agenda with diligence and excellence.

7. Thanksgiving: The Culture of Grateful Believers.

Thanksgiving is not an event; it is a lifestyle. We teach the mystery of gratitude as a key that opens doors, sustains blessings, and attracts divine intervention. A thankful believer is a victorious believer.

8. The Holy Spirit: Our Guide, Teacher, and Strength.

Our ministry deeply emphasizes the person and ministry of the Holy Spirit. Through His power we pray, we witness, we understand Scripture, we minister, and we overcome. We teach believers to cultivate intimacy with the Spirit and walk in His gifts and fruits.

9. Prayer and Fasting: Catalysts for Power and Breakthrough.

Prayer is communion; fasting is consecration. Together they produce spiritual sensitivity, deliverance, transformation, and revival. Our teachings guide believers into effective, fervent, and strategic prayer that produces results.

10. Kingdom Mandate, Evangelism, and Missions.

We exist to make Christ known. Our mandate is to take the gospel beyond our walls—into streets, cities, nations, the digital space, and unreached territories.

We train and deploy soul-winners, missionaries, and ministers who carry the burning passion of Christ for the lost.

The Kingdom expands wherever believers boldly proclaim the message of salvation.

11. Consecration: A Life Separated unto God

The pursuit of God requires purity and devotion. We teach the lifestyle of consecration, holiness, obedience, and spiritual discipline that preserves the fire of God on the altar of a believer’s heart.

12. VISION OR DIVINE PURPOSE.

Every life on earth is born with purpose, but only those who discover God’s vision for their lives experience true fulfillment. Vision is not ambition, neither is it mere desire. Vision is Heaven’s revelation of your assignment on earth—a picture of the future God prepared for you before you were born.

When a believer aligns with God’s purpose, life gains direction, clarity, and meaning. Confusion disappears. Decisions become intentional. Strength is renewed. Vision gives you the courage to persevere and the discipline to grow. It becomes the compass that guides your choices and the fire that fuels your pursuit.

Living according to God’s vision begins with intimacy. As we pray, study the Word, and yield to the Holy Ghost, God unveils His plan, step by step. He shows us who we are, what we are called to do, and the paths we must take. Purpose becomes clearer as we walk with Him, obeying His instructions and trusting His timing.

A life without vision drifts, but a life anchored in God’s purpose flourishes. When we live by divine vision, we live intentionally. We invest our gifts wisely, we serve joyfully, and we bear fruit that lasts. We become channels through which God touches the world.

Your life is not an accident. You are God’s idea, crafted for a unique assignment. Choose today to seek His direction, embrace His purpose, and walk boldly in the vision He has designed for you. In His purpose, you will find strength, fulfillment, and lasting impact.

13. Honour as an Inheritance

Honour is not just a virtue; it is a blood-bought inheritance of the believer. In Revelation 5:12, honour is listed among the seven redemptive rights Jesus obtained for us through His death, burial, and resurrection. This means honour is part of our spiritual identity and covenant heritage in Christ.
